The people behind the sound

Meet the owners.

We are friends who found common ground in music and vintage audio.

What began with swapping records and tinkering with gear became a way to share that enthusiasm with other listeners in Boise. We want the shop to feel approachable: a place to ask questions, hear a favorite track, and find something that feels right for you.

Great audio is best shared. That idea still guides every conversation.
