Service information

Keep the music playing.

Information about vintage audio repair at Boise Hi-Fi Stereo.

What we service

We repair vintage audio gear from any brand — receivers, amplifiers, turntables, tuners, speakers, and more.

Starting a repair inquiry

Email us using the contact details below to start a repair inquiry. Diagnosis and estimates are free.

When reaching out about a piece of equipment, include its brand, model number, symptoms, and any service history you know. Photos can help explain a problem.

Do not bring equipment to the shop until a drop-off has been arranged.
