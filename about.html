Our story

A friendship built around music.

Boise Hi-Fi Stereo began with friends sharing records, comparing gear, and chasing the sound that makes music feel close.

Why we do this

We started Boise Hi-Fi Stereo to make finding classic audio equipment more personal. A good system is about more than a list of specifications. It should fit your room, your records, and the way you like to listen.

We love the stories behind vintage gear as much as the music it plays. If you are building your first setup or adding to an old favorite, we are glad to talk through the possibilities with you.
